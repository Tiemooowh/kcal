KCal Support

A calorie and macro tracker for iPhone. If something is wrong or missing, write to timothy.de.mey@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you are on. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Open the app and start logging. There is nothing to sign up for.
Where is my data kept?
On the phone, in the app's own database, which is included in your iPhone backup. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you set up a backup server of your own in Settings.
Why does a meal say "estimate"?
Because nothing measured it. A row from a barcode or from your pantry carries values somebody recorded; an estimate is a considered guess at a portion. Every row says which it is, and you can correct any of them by hand.
A row is flagged and counted as zero.
Its energy does not match its macros, so the app will not add it up silently. Open the row, correct the values, and it counts again. Wine and beer usually need their alcohol filled in; sugar-free products need their polyols.
Plate measurement does not work.
It needs the depth camera, so it runs on iPhones with LiDAR or a dual camera, and only on the device itself. Photos taken from your library carry no depth. Everything else works regardless.
Apple Health is not showing up.
Open Settings in the app, tap Apple Health, and allow the types it asks for. iOS only shows that sheet for types it has never been asked about, so if nothing appears, turn them on in the Health app under Sharing › Apps › KCal.
How do I delete everything?
Delete the app. That removes the database with it. There is no copy on a server unless you configured one yourself, and you control that server.

Reporting a problem

Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps more than a description of the screen. If a meal came out wrong, the text or photo you logged is the most useful thing to send.

KCal is not a medical device. Calorie and macro values are estimates, for information only, and are not medical or dietary advice.
