KCal Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

KCal is a calorie and macro tracker that runs on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. This policy describes every case in which data leaves your device, and there are only two, both of which you switch on yourself.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you log is written to a database inside the app on your phone: meals and their ingredients, portions, photographs you attach, your pantry, your weight, and the activity read from Apple Health. It is included in your iPhone's own backup, encrypted to the same standard as the rest of the device. It is not uploaded anywhere by default.

Apple Health

If you allow it, KCal reads steps, workouts, active and resting energy, and body mass, and writes back the energy, macros, water and caffeine of the meals you log. Health data is used only to work out your daily budget and to show what you burned. It is never sent off the device, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone. You can withdraw either direction at any time in the Health app, under Sharing › Apps › KCal.

When an estimate is made

Describing a meal in words, or photographing a plate, sends that text or that photograph to an AI endpoint you configure in Settings, which returns the estimated ingredients. Nothing else goes with it: no identifier, no name, no health data, no history. If no endpoint is configured, no request is made and the feature says so. Barcode lookups query the Open Food Facts database with the barcode number alone. The nutrition-label reader runs entirely on the phone.

Your own backup server

KCal can copy its database to a server you run and point it at. That is off unless you enter a server address yourself, and the server is yours: this app's author neither operates it nor has access to it.

What is never collected

  • No account, email address or name.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No location.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its database. Meals written to Apple Health stay in Health, where you can remove them yourself. If you configured a backup server, deleting data there is yours to do.

Children

KCal is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone.
